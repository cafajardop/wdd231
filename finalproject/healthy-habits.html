Healthy Habits

No motivational circus. Just small habits that are easy to repeat and actually fit into real life.

Hydration

Keep water visible. If it’s out of sight, it’s basically a myth.

  • Goal: 6–8 cups/day
  • Trigger: drink before coffee
  • Tip: reuse a bottle
  • Why: energy + focus

Daily Movement

Walking counts. Stretching counts. “Something” beats “nothing” every time.

  • Goal: 15 minutes
  • Trigger: after lunch
  • Tip: stairs > elevator
  • Why: mood + stamina

Sleep Basics

Consistency matters more than perfection. Protect the last hour of your day.

  • Goal: same bedtime
  • Trigger: phone down
  • Tip: dim lights
  • Why: recovery + cravings

A Simple Habit Plan

Pick one habit, set a tiny goal, and repeat it for a week.

  • Choose one habit (ex: 10-min walk)
  • Make it tiny (ex: 5 min is allowed)
  • Attach it to a trigger (after lunch)
  • Track it in the Toolkit

Pro Tip

Lower the bar until you can’t trip over it. Then you’ll actually do it.

Consistency is the cheat code.
