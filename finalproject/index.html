Quick Routines

Short routines that fit into real life. Pick one, do it today, and call it a win.

10-Min Stretch Reset

Loosen shoulders, hips, and hamstrings. Great for desk days.

  • Time: 10 min
  • Level: Beginner

Bodyweight Basics

Squats, push-ups (modified allowed), and a plank. Simple and effective.

  • Time: 12–15 min
  • Level: Beginner–Intermediate

Walk + Hydrate

A short walk plus a full glass of water. Low effort, high payoff.

  • Time: 15 min
  • Level: Any

Healthy Habits

Habits aren’t motivation. They’re design. Make the good choice the easy choice.

  • Daily movement: walk, stretch, or a quick routine
  • Hydration: keep water visible (yes, it matters)
  • Sleep basics: consistent bedtime when possible
  • Micro-breaks: 2 minutes every hour to reset
